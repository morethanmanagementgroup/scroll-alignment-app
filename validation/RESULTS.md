## 7. Local birth time → UTC

6,808 cases: 3,000 random (zone, local time) pairs 1900–2030 plus every 15 minutes within ±2 h of clock changes in 400 random zone-years. Reference: Python zoneinfo (system tzdata 2026e), an independent implementation. Scroll: bundled IANA table 2026e+backzone (data/tz-transitions.json).

| Reference says | Cases | Scroll agrees |
|---|---|---|
| one instant | 5,917 | 5,917 |
| skipped (clocks went forward) | 438 | 438 |
| ambiguous (clocks went back) | 453 | 453 |

Why Scroll ships its own zone table: with Node's built-in ICU zone data (tz 2025b), 145 of 5,917 unambiguous cases would resolve to a different instant (145 of them before 1970), because ICU omits IANA backzone history — e.g. Stockholm in 1947 gets Berlin's daylight saving (UTC+2) instead of Swedish time (UTC+1), which would move every line about 15°. The reference and Scroll share IANA source data, so this check validates the conversion logic (gaps, folds, offsets with seconds) rather than the historical records themselves.
